After a wonderful six months in Cork with [Qualcomm](https://www.qualcomm.com/), I've relocated to Stockholm to complete the second year of my [EIT Digital](https://www.eitdigital.eu) double degree at [KTH Royal Institute of Technology](https://www.kth.se).

Ireland treated me well, both professionally and personally, and I'm grateful for the time spent there. Now it's time for a new city, a new routine, and a thesis I'm genuinely enjoying digging into.

Settling in also frees up time and headspace to push forward on [RealityFoundation](https://realityfoundation.eu), the project I co-founded to study the boundaries between authentic and synthetic media. More to come soon.
