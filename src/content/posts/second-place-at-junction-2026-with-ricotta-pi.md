This weekend at [Junction 2026](https://hackjunction.app/hackathons/junction-2026), [Chiara Marangoni](https://www.linkedin.com/in/chiara-marangoni-2a785318b/), [Dennis Orlando](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dennis-orlando-06019519b/), and I won second place in the Intel Physical AI Challenge with [Ricotta Pi](https://github.com/emiliodallatorre/hackjunction2026-intel). Our SO-101 robot inspects LEGO DUPLO bricks for defects and sorts them accordingly.

We fine-tuned Physical Intelligence's π0.5 vision-language-action model on NVIDIA B200 GPUs through Modal. During the demonstration, π0.5 ran on the Intel Core Ultra X9 388H's integrated GPU while PatchCore checked for defects on its NPU, both using OpenVINO.
