I am pleased to announce my acceptance into the [EIT Digital Master School](https://www.eitdigital.eu) double degree program.

I will commence my studies at Politecnico di Milano with a focus on [High Performance Computing Engineering](https://masterhpc.polimi.it), subsequently continuing at KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm specializing in [ICT Innovation](https://www.kth.se/en/studies/master/ict-innovation-eit/msc-ict-innovation-eit-digital-1.357727).

EIT Digital is a prestigious European initiative supported by the [European Institute of Innovation & Technology (EIT)](https://www.eit.europa.eu), dedicated to developing top-tier digital talent through the combination of advanced technical education and entrepreneurial training. The program also features summer schools across Europe, offering students additional opportunities to deepen their expertise and connect with peers from leading institutions.
